The problem

One AI question keeps moving from one meeting to the next.

A teacher asks what is allowed. A parent asks what happens to student information. Someone finds a new tool, somebody else finds a policy problem, and the question gets pushed to the next meeting.

The Design Lab gives the people who carry the decision a structured place to work through it together.

How the ninety minutes works

This is working time.

01 · Understand

Identify the people affected and the assumptions already in the room.

02 · Define

Name the real decision instead of solving the loudest version of it.

03 · Generate

Consider possible boundaries and the tradeoffs each one creates.

04 · Prototype

Build a draft decision card with an owner, missing evidence, and a next check.

05 · Test

Change a condition and see where the proposed boundary breaks.

06 · Commit

Name what your local review process needs to do next.

What your team leaves with

Something useful enough to carry into the next meeting.

Up to three draft decision cards

Each names a proposed boundary, a review-owner role, missing evidence, and the smallest useful next check.

A one-page cover note

We clean the cards after the session and return them with a concise summary.

One correction round

Your team can consolidate factual corrections before the package is final.

The cards support your local review process. They do not approve a product, replace legal or privacy review, or write district policy.
