Questions people bring

Useful questions are a better starting point than more tools.

I use AI all the time. Why has it not changed anything about my work?

Using a tool often and getting value from it are different. We look for where AI genuinely removes friction, where it adds steps, and where one small change can give you time or energy back.

Can I put this client or business information into an AI tool?

It depends on the information, your agreements, your profession, and the responsibility you carry. We can map the decision and the workflow; your attorney, regulator, or compliance professional remains the authority where formal advice is required.

How do I use AI without sounding like everybody else?

Your voice and experience are part of what people hire. We work from your real writing and examples, then build review points so the result still sounds like a person your clients know.

What should I keep doing myself?

AI can help with repetitive or preparatory work, but it cannot own your relationships or your judgment. We draw that line explicitly instead of quietly automating past it.

I am still planning my business. Do I have enough “real work” to bring?

Yes. Bring the offer you are shaping, the customer problem you are trying to understand, or the operating work you expect to repeat. We will choose one useful artifact or decision to move forward.

What is the difference between a custom GPT and an agent?

A custom GPT helps you have a more consistent conversation with AI. An agent can take on a defined piece of work and bring it back for review. The useful question is not which label sounds newer; it is what work should move forward while you remain responsible for the decisions.
