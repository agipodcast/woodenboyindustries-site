Inside the Ivy Tech IT Challenge

You're sitting next to students you just met, from schools and programs that aren't yours. There's an employer-designed challenge on the table. Now you have to figure out how to work together.

THE ROOM WAS THE POINT

01 / THE EXPERIENCE GAP

Experience has to start somewhere.

The opening made the setup plain: work with people outside your program, then talk to the employers who do the work. Students outside the championship could use the afternoon for a career fair, résumé reviews and mock interviews.

BUILD EXPERIENCE, THEN TALK ABOUT IT

02 / SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT & COMPUTER SCIENCE

What would you build first?

Software teams had to turn an idea into something they could show. The useful conversation starts before the code: what should this do, and what's the smallest version that would convince another person it works?

What was the Ivy Tech IT Challenge?

A statewide, one-day competition in Indianapolis on November 20, 2026. Students worked on employer-designed challenges in teams across Indiana colleges.

How were students grouped?

The opening explained that many students would team up with people from different schools and programs. The championship regrouped preliminary winners.

What was Operation First Light?

The afternoon interdisciplinary challenge asked teams to commission a fictional data campus, with each discipline depending on the others. No results are included here.

What could students do outside the championship?

The program included a career fair, résumé reviews, mock interviews and employer networking.

Who sponsored the challenge?

Google: presenting. TechPoint: partner. Elanco, Elevance Health, IBM and LRS: challenge sponsors. First Internet Bank: breakfast.
